Drift diagnostic

Drift looks like momentum from outside and feels like motion without meaning from inside. Tick the signs you recognise. Two or more mean the team has lost focus — then choose one immediate action and do it this week.

    What to notice

    • Every sign is a symptom of a decision someone avoided — too many priorities, a tough call not made, trade-offs not named.
    • Immediate actions are small and concrete (one six-week goal, a “stop doing” list); sustained practices keep drift from returning.
    • The book’s rule is one action this week, not a programme: even several signs call for one move first.
