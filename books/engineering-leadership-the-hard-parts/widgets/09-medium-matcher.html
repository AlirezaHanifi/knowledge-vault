Medium matcher

Different information flows best through different channels. For each message, pick the medium you’d use. The book rarely names a single right answer, so several choices can match — and for what matters most, the answer is to multicast.

Illustrates “Mediums and modalities” through “Documentation”, pp. 180–187. Each medium’s strengths and the examples are the book’s; turning them into a matching exercise, and which media count as a match, are the vault’s.

    What to notice

    • Video is for emotion, relationships and complex discussion — and broadly terrible for detailed technical work unless structured. Avoid slides.
    • Audio is underused: a two-minute voice note carries both a technical concern and a supportive tone.
    • Email still wins where you need formality, a paper trail or to cross organizational boundaries — but a thread of ten or more emails wants another medium.
    • Spamming is the same message through the same medium again and again. Multicasting — email, chat, the doc and a call — is how an important message actually lands.
