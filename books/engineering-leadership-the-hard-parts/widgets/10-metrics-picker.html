Metrics picker

Treat metrics as a product: start from the question your customers are really asking, not the one they typed. Then build a small set — the four DORA metrics plus one or two for your biggest pain point.

      What to notice

      • Questions like “who commits the most?” aren’t stupid; they are attempts to quantify an unfamiliar domain. Ask what decision the answer would change.
      • Many questions boil down to “Are we okay?” — will we meet objectives, are we getting value for money, is quality good enough?
      • DORA balances speed (deployment frequency, lead time) with stability (change failure rate, time to restore). Five or six metrics is enough to act on.
      • Measure teams, not individuals, and tell people what you are not measuring, so invisible work isn’t crowded out.
