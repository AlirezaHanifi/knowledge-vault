Cold path, hot path

A traditional system acquires resources before events arrive (acquire → receive → process → release). A serverless system acquires them after (receive → acquire → process → release). Real platforms keep resources for a while after an event, so a later event takes the hot path and skips acquisition; otherwise it takes the cold path.

    What to notice

    • Traditional: no event waits for acquisition, but the instances are paid for the whole time. Too few, and bursts queue; too many, and most resource-seconds are idle.
    • Serverless, keep-alive 0: every event takes the cold path, and nothing is held idle.
    • Longer keep-alive: events inside a burst find a warm instance and take the hot path, at the price of instances held idle between bursts.
