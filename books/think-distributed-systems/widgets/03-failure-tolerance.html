Masking, nonmasking and fail-safe failure tolerance

Failure tolerance is classified by what survives a failure. Masking keeps safety and liveness. Nonmasking keeps liveness only: it keeps going but may make mistakes. Fail-safe keeps safety only: it makes no mistakes but may stop.

    What to notice

    • Listing 3.1 is nonmasking. At x = 0 safety (x ∈ {1, 2}) is violated, but the repair transition 0 → 1 keeps it live, and both legal values keep recurring.
    • x = 3 is intolerable. No transition leaves it, so nothing guarantees a return to a legal or illegal state.
    • The nonmasking queue keeps delivering and delivers message 3 out of order once the node recovers. The fail-safe queue delivers nothing during the failure, then catches up in order. Masking hides the failure entirely, at the price of another copy of every message.
