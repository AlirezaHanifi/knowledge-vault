Raft’s election restriction and the three puzzles

Raft elects only a candidate whose log is at least as up to date as a quorum’s: the later last term wins, and with equal last terms the longer log wins. Because any two quorums intersect, a committed entry is in every future leader’s log. An entry that isn’t committed yet may be kept or lost, depending on who can win.

    What to notice

    • Puzzle 1: node 2 has the longest log, so it is the leader of term 3: the leader appends client requests first.
    • Puzzle 2: crash node 2. Nodes 1 and 3 never saw request 9, so whoever wins term 4 doesn’t have it, and when node 2 recovers its 9 is overwritten. That is allowed, since 9 was never committed.
    • Puzzle 3: node 1 holds 9 too. Crash node 2 and only node 1 can win, because node 3’s log is less up to date. Node 1 replicates 9 to node 3 and commits it.
    • Build your own logs with + and −, and check that a candidate can win only if a quorum of live nodes, itself included, has logs no more up to date than its own.
