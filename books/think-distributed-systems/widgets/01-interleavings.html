Interleavings and race conditions

A process is a sequence of atomic actions. Two concurrent processes P and Q can run in any interleaving of their actions; (P | Q) is the set of all of them. A race condition exists when some of those interleavings are considered incorrect. The process pair is serializable if every interleaving has the same effect as P·Q or Q·P.

    What to notice

    • With read, update and write as separate actions, only the two serial interleavings give the serial result. In the other 18, one process reads before the other writes, and one update is lost.
    • Making the update one atomic action leaves only two interleavings, P·Q and Q·P, so there’s no race.
    • With +1 and ×2 the two serial orders give different results (4 and 3). Both are correct under serializability; anything else is not.
