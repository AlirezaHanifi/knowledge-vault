Free shipping over $50

“Shipping should be free on all orders costing $50 or more.” It sounds complete, but it isn’t. Does the $50 include tax? Shipping? Ebooks? Your job is to find the edge cases and feed the consequences back, then let the client decide.

Illustrates “Programming as Therapy” in Topic 45, The Requirements Pit (pp. 406–409): the rule, the developer’s questions, and the $25 book with about $30 of overnight shipping. Prices of the other items and shipping options, and the 8% tax, are the vault’s. Implementation is the vault’s own.

    What to notice

    • Counting shipping toward the $50 is simple to explain, but it lets a $25 book with $30 of overnight shipping become a free-shipping order: overnight delivery of a $25 book for $25.
    • Every answer to the developer’s questions changes the set of orders that qualify. None of them is “wrong”: they are decisions the client hadn’t realized they were making.
    • Deliver facts, then stop. The page shows what each choice costs; choosing which rule they want is the client’s job.
