Team size and communication paths

A pragmatic team is small, under 10–12 people, and stable. Fifty people aren’t a team; they’re a horde. One reason, from the book’s footnote: communication paths grow much faster than the number of people, until communication breaks down.

Illustrates Topic 49, Pragmatic Teams (pp. 437–438, Tip 84, and footnote 75 on p. 467). The pair count n(n − 1)/2 is the standard formula for the growth the footnote describes. Splitting into teams that talk through one liaison each is the vault’s model. Implementation is the vault’s own.

    What to notice

    • Each new person adds a path to everyone already there: 10 people have 45 paths, 12 have 66, and 50 have 1,225.
    • Small stable teams keep that number low enough that everyone can know and trust everyone else.
    • Splitting a horde into teams cuts the paths sharply, but only if the teams are fully functional (Tip 86), so most work doesn’t need the paths between teams.
