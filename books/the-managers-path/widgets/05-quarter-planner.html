Quarter capacity planner

Plan with rules of thumb: about 10 productive weeks per engineer per quarter, 20% for sustaining engineering, and double your quick estimates. Then see what actually fits.

Illustrates “Project Management Rules of Thumb” (pp. 93–95). The project list and its estimates are invented, and so is the size of the quarter-to-quarter difference: the book says only that Q1 tends to be most productive and Q4 least. Implementation is the vault’s own.

    What to notice

    • Thirteen calendar weeks are about ten productive ones: vacations, meetings, review season, outages and onboarding take the rest.
    • Untick “double” and everything fits — on paper. Quick estimates are optimistic, which is why the book doubles them.
    • Setting sustaining work to 0% frees a project’s worth of time this quarter, and the book warns that scheduling features to 100% slows features down.
