Chapter 3 · Tech Lead

Maker’s schedule, manager’s schedule

The tech lead’s “one weird trick” is balance: step away from the code when the team needs you, but give yourself — and the team — blocks of uninterrupted time. The worst mistake is getting pulled randomly into meetings.

Illustrates “All Great Tech Leads Know This One Weird Trick” (p. 31). The calendar, the presets and the minimum useful block length are the vault’s; the book gives no block length. Implementation is the vault’s own.

    What to notice

    • The presets hold the same eight meeting hours. Scattered, they leave almost no block long enough for deep work; batched, most of the week survives.
    • A single meeting in the middle of an afternoon destroys more focus time than its own hour.
    • The book asks the tech lead to break their own work into pieces that don’t need multi-day focus, while getting the team a schedule that does (p. 31).
