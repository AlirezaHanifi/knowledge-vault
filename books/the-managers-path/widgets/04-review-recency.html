Whole year or last two months?

A performance review should account for the whole year, not the last couple of months. Keep running 1-1 notes, or reconstruct the year month by month from email and launches, so early wins and growth since then both get credit.

Illustrates “Writing and Delivering a Performance Review” (pp. 64–65). The monthly scores and the exponential memory curve are invented, a simple model of recency bias; the book doesn’t quantify it. Implementation is the vault’s own.

    What to notice

    • A strong first half fades from memory: an engineer who shipped the year’s biggest project in spring is rated on a quiet autumn.
    • A late bloomer is flattered — memory rates the person they became, not the year they had.
    • Notes make every month count equally. The longer your memory’s reach (the slider), the smaller the gap, but nobody remembers twelve months evenly.
