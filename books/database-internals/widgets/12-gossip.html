Gossip: spreading a rumour like an epidemic

Gossip spreads information like an epidemic. A node that has news is infective and each round pushes it to fanout random peers. Susceptible nodes that receive it become infective too. Messages to nodes that already know are redundant. Interest loss eventually makes a node removed, so it stops spreading: either with some probability every round, or after it has hit a threshold of nodes that already knew.

    What to notice

    • Spread is logarithmic. Infective counts roughly multiply each round at first, so 64 nodes are covered in a handful of rounds.
    • Redundancy is the price of robustness. Once most nodes know, almost every message lands on a node that already knew.
    • Interest loss trades coverage for messages. Lose interest too quickly and the rumour dies out before reaching everyone: watch “never reached”.
    • Fanout matters most early. A larger fanout reaches everyone sooner and survives aggressive interest loss, at the cost of more messages per round.
