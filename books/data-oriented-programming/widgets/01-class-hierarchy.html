The “tiny” feature

Nancy’s late requirements: VIP members may add book items, and Super members may list the books lent to other members. In Theo’s class hierarchy each right is locked in a class, so methods move into new base classes and diamonds appear. In the DOP design, each right is one more function.

Illustrates section 1.2.4, Complex class hierarchies (figures 1.16–1.17, pp. 20–24), and section 2.5, DOP systems are flexible (listings 2.3–2.6, pp. 39–41). The exact arrows for the Super-member hierarchy are the vault’s reading of figure 1.17, chosen to match its three diamonds. Implementation is the vault’s own.
