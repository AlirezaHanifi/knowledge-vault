Capture and replay

In DOP the function context is just the arguments, and arguments are data. Serialize them to JSON at the top of the function, paste them into a REPL, and you can replay the call and improve the code there, with a much shorter feedback loop than triggering the endpoint.

Illustrates sections 15.2–15.4 (listings 15.4–15.26, pp. 314–328): console.log versus JSON.stringify on a string with quotes, the captured catalog of listing 15.9, and three searches: lowercase startsWith on the title (listing 15.8), hasWordStartingWith on words (listings 15.3 and 15.12), and the lowercase \b regular expression (listing 15.24). The eight queries are listings 15.25–15.26’s. The REPL is a mock. Implementation is the vault’s own.

    What to notice
    
  
      
    
    • console.log drops the quotes around a string and the backslashes before inner quotes, so what you copy isn’t a valid literal. JSON keeps both, and works for any data.
      • 
    
    • The replay uses the captured data, not the live system: change the endpoint query, recapture, and the REPL works on what was captured.
      • 
    
    • Only the third version passes all eight queries: lowercasing fixes “habit”, and the word-boundary regular expression handles prefixes that span words, like “7 Habit” and “habits of”.
