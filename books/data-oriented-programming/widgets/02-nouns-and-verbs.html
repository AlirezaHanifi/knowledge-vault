Nouns and verbs

DOP design starts with the data: highlight the nouns and noun phrases in the requirements to find data entities, and group them. Then highlight the verb phrases to find the functionality, which becomes stateless functions in code modules.

          What to notice

          • Nouns become data entities, grouped into two high-level groups: the catalog (books, authors, book items, book lendings) and user management (users, members, librarians).
          • Verb phrases become functions, plus two the requirements imply: returning a book and checking whether a user is a librarian.
          • The modules follow the data: Catalog and UserManagement match the two groups of the mind map, and every function receives its data as an explicit first argument.
