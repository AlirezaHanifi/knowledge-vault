Nest or merge

Two sources describe the same book. You can nest one inside the other, as an extendedInfo field, and never worry about conflicts, or merge them into one flat map and handle fields both sides define. Either way, the information is passed forward as data, unopened.

Illustrates section 11.5, Passing information forward (table 11.1 and listings 11.10–11.12, pp. 231–233), and the conflicting titles of listing 11.19 (p. 238). The extra shared fields are the vault’s. Implementation is the vault’s own.

    What to notice
    
  
      
    
    • Nesting never conflicts, but the result is not flat: the client must know to look inside extendedInfo.
      • 
    
    • Merging is flat, and safe as long as the two maps share no fields. In listing 11.19 they share title, with different values, and the side merged last wins silently.
      • 
    
    • Hugo’s letter: neither option opens the information. Data is passed forward in generic structures, without a class per source.
