Author names by prolificity

Nancy’s “tiny” feature: show an author’s name in italic, bold, or both, depending on how many books they wrote, in HTML or Markdown. Dave recognizes dynamic dispatch: the dispatch function emits [prolificity level, format], and six small methods do the formatting.

Illustrates section 13.5 (table 13.1; listings 13.23–13.29, pp. 289–293): Author.prolificityLevel (≤ 10 low, ≥ 51 high, otherwise medium), authorNameDispatch, and the six HTML and Markdown methods. The book counts are the vault’s slider; the default author is the book’s test. Implementation is the vault’s own.
