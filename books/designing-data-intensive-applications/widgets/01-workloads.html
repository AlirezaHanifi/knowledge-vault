Sort the workloads

Two distinctions run through the whole book: operational versus analytical workloads, and systems of record versus derived data. Classify each item; the feedback names the characteristic that decides it.

Illustrates “Characterizing Transaction Processing and Analytics” (pp. 5–7, Table 1-1) and “Systems of Record and Derived Data” (pp. 10–11). The example items are the vault’s own.

What to notice

  • The access pattern decides, not the data. The same orders table serves point lookups (operational) and year-long aggregates (analytical).
  • Who asks matters. Operational queries come from end users through an application; analytical queries come from analysts and data scientists supporting decisions.
  • Derived data can be thrown away and rebuilt. If you can recompute it from somewhere else, it isn’t the system of record — even if it is a copy you rely on every day.
